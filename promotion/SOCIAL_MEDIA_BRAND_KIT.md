| Element | Hex Color | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Circular Coin Face** | `#101D35` | Deep navy coin base with subtle radial depth; 100% transparent exterior |

| **Central Geometric Letter `M`** | `#EAC77B` | Primary bold geometric `M` with flat baseline + nested parallel inner `M` |
